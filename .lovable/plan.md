# Make the case easier to follow

## What will change
- Replace the dense goal strip with a plain-language case summary and one highlighted “Do this next” action.
- Make the active suspect and their stress immediately understandable on small screens.
- Keep clues visible, but show only the essential clue names first; details remain available when selected.
- Reduce the questioning controls to the main question action, with Cold Read, Press, and Set a Trap grouped under one optional “More tactics” control.
- Move progress details and secondary tools behind compact controls so they do not compete with the investigation.
- Shorten the opening instructions to three simple steps using the same language as the main screen.

## What stays the same
- Both complete cases, AI interviews, crime-scene hotspots, victim interviews, clues, accusation flow, saved progress, Jane’s Intuition, tea, My Desk, music, and all Mentalist extras.
- The dark navy, charcoal, warm amber, cinematic identity, and single-screen layout.

## Verification
- Check the first-time briefing and first guided action.
- Play the main loop on phone and desktop sizes.
- Confirm clue selection, suspect switching, questioning, crime scene, and Solve Case remain available without overlap or errors.
